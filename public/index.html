What Car Crash Lawyer AI does

Car Crash Lawyer AI is a UK road traffic accident app that helps you record evidence at the scene and turns it into a complete, professionally formatted incident report. Instead of trying to remember details days later, you capture them in the minutes that matter: a spoken account of what happened, photographs of the damage and the scene, your exact location, and the other driver's vehicle and insurance details.

Your voice statement is transcribed and structured automatically, your photographs are attached to the right sections, and the finished 18-page PDF report is emailed to you — typically within a couple of minutes of finishing the form. You can then pass it to your insurer or your solicitor.

Who it is for

It is built for UK drivers: everyday motorists, learner and newly qualified drivers, and employers responsible for staff who drive for work. It is equally useful whether the collision was your fault, someone else's, or is disputed — the point is that the evidence is recorded properly and consistently either way.

Car Crash Lawyer AI is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. It documents your accident so that whoever advises you has the facts.

How it works

  1. Set up your profile once. Add your driver, vehicle and insurance details in advance, so that after a collision you are not hunting for a policy number at the roadside.
  2. Check everyone is safe. The app opens with emergency access to 999 and shares your precise location, including a what3words address, with the emergency services.
  3. Record what happened. Describe the collision in your own words. The recording is transcribed and organised into a written statement.
  4. Capture the evidence. Photograph the damage, the scene and the other vehicle. Add the other driver, any witnesses, police details and dashcam footage. A DVLA lookup confirms the other vehicle's make, model, MOT and tax status from its registration.
  5. Get your report. The app assembles everything into an 18-page PDF incident report and emails it to you, ready to send to your insurer or solicitor.

What it costs

A subscription costs £11.99 a year and can be cancelled at any time. That covers unlimited incident reports, evidence storage, AI transcription and your dashboard. There is no charge per report and no fee at the point you need it.

Your data and privacy

Car Crash Lawyer AI is UK GDPR compliant. Your data is encrypted at rest and processed entirely within our own platform — it is not passed to third-party AI services. The app does not track your location in the background; GPS and what3words are used only when you choose to report an incident. You can export or delete your data at any time.
