Permission Required

To use this feature, please enable permissions.

📱 Enable in App Settings

This app needs permission to access your microphone.

  1. Tap "Open App Settings" below
  2. Tap Permissions
  3. Enable the required permission
  4. Return to this app

📱 Android Settings

  1. Open your device Settings
  2. Tap Apps or Application Manager
  3. Find and tap your browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.)
  4. Tap Permissions
  5. Enable the required permissions:
    • Camera - For accident photos
    • Microphone - For voice recording
    • Location - For incident location
  6. Return to this app and tap "Try Again" below

📱 iOS Settings

  1. Open your device Settings
  2. Scroll down and tap Car Crash Lawyer AI
  3. Enable Camera, Microphone, and Location access
  4. Return to this app and tap "Try Again" below

💻 Browser Settings

Chrome/Edge:

  1. Click the lock icon in the address bar
  2. Click "Site settings"
  3. Change Camera, Microphone, and Location to "Allow"
  4. Refresh this page

Firefox:

  1. Click the shield icon in the address bar
  2. Click "Permissions"
  3. Enable Camera, Microphone, and Location
  4. Refresh this page
