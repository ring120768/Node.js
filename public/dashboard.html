🔒 What Data We Store

▼

👤 Personal Information

  • Driving license details

Used for: Account creation, legal document generation

  • Vehicle registration, make, model, colour
  • Insurance company name and policy number

Used for: DVLA lookups, incident reporting

📸 Incident Documentation

  • Accident photos and damage images
  • Voice recordings of witness statements
  • Incident details (date, location, description)

Used for: Legal evidence, insurance claims

Used for: Providing you with legal documentation
