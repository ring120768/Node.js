Using what3words to Report Your Exact Accident Location

"Somewhere on the A34 past Newbury" can cover fifteen miles of dual carriageway with a barrier down the middle. A what3words address narrows it to a three-metre square, and most UK emergency services accept one on a 999 call. Give it alongside the road and direction, never instead of them.

Last updated 5 August 2026 · Applies across the UK

What it is

what3words divides the world into three-metre squares and gives each one a unique combination of three words. Instead of reading out coordinates, you say three ordinary words and the call handler sees the exact square.

It matters after a crash because the two hardest questions on a 999 call are often "where are you?" and "which side?" — and a shaken driver on an unfamiliar road frequently cannot answer either.

How widely it is accepted

what3words states that over 85% of UK police, ambulance and fire services use it, and that call handlers accept three word addresses on 999 calls. It is used by a range of responders including the UK Coastguard.

85% is not everyone, and this is what3words' own figure. Their guidance is to gather as much location information as you can, in case a three word address cannot be accepted on that particular call. Treat it as a powerful addition to conventional location details, not a replacement for them.

What to say on the call

Lead with what every call handler can use, then add the precision:

  1. Road and direction first

    "M40 northbound" or "A34 southbound towards Winchester." Direction is critical on any dual carriageway or motorway — the wrong side can mean miles of detour for responders.

  2. Nearest fixed reference

    The last junction you passed, a driver location sign, a marker post, a bridge, a village name, a house number. Motorway driver location signs give the road, direction and distance and are designed for exactly this.

  3. Then the three words

    Say them clearly and offer to spell any that could be misheard. Confirm the handler has read them back correctly.

  4. What they are coming to

    How many vehicles, how many people, whether anyone is trapped or unconscious, whether the road is blocked, and whether there is fuel spillage or fire risk. This determines what gets sent.

Does it work without signal?

The app can work out your three word address offline — it converts your phone's GPS position using an algorithm stored on the device, rather than looking it up over the internet. GPS itself does not need mobile data.

Two caveats, both practical:

  • You still need enough signal to make the 999 call. In the UK, 999 can connect via any available network, not only your own.
  • You need the app already installed. You cannot download it with no data, and nobody installs an app while standing in a live carriageway.

How accurate is it really?

Each address identifies a three-metre square — but that is the resolution of the grid, not a guarantee about your phone. The square you are given is only as good as the GPS fix behind it, which can drift under heavy tree cover, between tall buildings, in tunnels or multi-storey car parks.

If the reported square looks wrong on the map, say so and fall back on landmarks. A handler would far rather have "outside the Red Lion on the B4009" than a confidently wrong three-metre square.

Beyond the 999 call

An exact location is worth recording for the claim as well. Months later, "the A34" tells an insurer nothing about the junction layout, the lane markings or the sightlines. A precise point lets anyone reading the file place the vehicles on a map and understand the geometry of what happened.

Record it alongside — never instead of — the road name and number, direction of travel, nearest junction, and the time. See our guide to documenting accident evidence for the rest of what to capture.

Set it up now, not later

  • Install the what3words app today, while you have data and a spare minute
  • Open it once so it has location permission and you know what the screen looks like
  • Learn the one gesture that shows your current address — under stress you will not explore a menu
  • Check your phone's location services are on

The location is one field of many

Knowing precisely where you are helps the ambulance find you. It does nothing for the claim that follows — that needs the photographs, the other driver's details, the witnesses and your account while it is still accurate.
