# ReprobusAI Logo Design Brief

**Project:** Corporate brand identity for ReprobusAI
**Date:** 2026-02-01
**Brand positioning:** B2B fleet safety and incident management platform powered by AI

---

## Brand Overview

### Company Name
**ReprobusAI** (can be styled as "Reprobus AI" or "ReprobusAI")

### Brand Story
ReprobusAI takes its name from the legendary figure who became St Christopher - the giant ferryman who carried travellers safely across dangerous waters. This ancient story of guardianship and safe passage translates perfectly to modern fleet management: we carry businesses safely through every journey, protecting their assets and people with AI-powered intelligence.

**Key narrative:** Ancient protection meets modern AI

### Target Audience
- **Primary:** UK corporate fleet managers, insurance companies, transport businesses
- **Secondary:** US enterprise clients, logistics companies
- **Decision makers:** CTOs, Risk Managers, Fleet Directors, Insurance Directors

### Brand Differentiation
ReprobusAI is the **corporate/B2B sister brand** to Car Crash Lawyer AI (consumer brand). Key differences:

| Aspect | Car Crash Lawyer AI | ReprobusAI |
|--------|-------------------|------------|
| Market | B2C (individuals) | B2B (corporate) |
| Focus | Post-incident legal support | Proactive fleet protection |
| Tone | Accessible, helpful | Professional, authoritative |
| Visual | Incident/crash imagery | Journey/protection imagery |
| Colour | Sky blue (#0ea5e9) | Deep teal/navy (differentiate) |

**Critical:** Logo must be visually distinct from CCLA while maintaining protection theme.

## Design Direction

### Core Concept: "The Ferryman's Watch"

**Theme:** Guardian monitoring and protecting journeys in progress (NOT responding to crashes)

**Visual metaphor:**
- Ancient: Reprobus carrying travellers across dangerous rivers
- Modern: AI monitoring fleet vehicles across dangerous roads

**Positioning:**
- Proactive (preventing incidents)
- Protective (guardian/shield)
- Intelligent (AI-powered)
- Corporate (enterprise-grade)

### Visual Elements to Include

**Primary symbol options:**

1. **Shield with journey imagery** (recommended)
   - Shield outline (protection, authority, badge)
   - Road/path flowing through or emerging from shield
   - Subtle monitoring/AI elements (circuit pattern, nodes, network)
   - Forward motion, not impact/crash

2. **Medallion/badge design**
   - Circular form (St Christopher medals)
   - Ring structure with central icon
   - Professional badge aesthetic
   - Tech overlay pattern

3. **Abstract guardian**
   - Protective arch/canopy over road
   - Overlapping coverage zones
   - Network/connectivity pattern
   - Minimal, sophisticated

### Visual Elements to AVOID

❌ Crashed or damaged vehicles
❌ Scales of justice (too similar to CCLA)
❌ Accident/impact imagery
❌ Literal St Christopher figure (religious overtones inappropriate for B2B)
❌ Sky blue colour (CCLA uses this)
❌ Gavel, legal symbols (consumer brand territory)

## Colour Palette

### Primary Options

**Option 1: Deep Teal + Silver** (Recommended)
- **Primary:** `#0E7490` (Deep Teal)
  - Rationale: Trust, water (ferryman story), distinct from CCLA
  - Usage: Main logo, headers, CTAs
- **Accent:** `#94A3B8` (Cool Silver)
  - Rationale: Technology, premium, AI
  - Usage: Secondary elements, tech overlays
- **Supporting:** `#F8FAFC` (Light gray), `#1E293B` (Charcoal)

**Option 2: Navy + Gold**
- **Primary:** `#1E3A5F` (Navy Blue)
  - Rationale: Corporate authority, insurance industry standard
- **Accent:** `#F59E0B` (Amber Gold)
  - Rationale: Premium positioning, guardian theme
- **Supporting:** `#FFFFFF` (White), `#475569` (Slate)

**Option 3: Charcoal + Cyan**
- **Primary:** `#334155` (Charcoal)
  - Rationale: Sophisticated, serious B2B
- **Accent:** `#06B6D4` (Cyan)
  - Rationale: Tech-forward, AI aesthetic
- **Supporting:** `#F1F5F9` (Cool white), `#0F172A` (Deep charcoal)

### Colour Requirements
- Must work in full colour, monochrome, and reversed (white on dark)
- Accessible contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum)
- Professional enough for corporate presentations
- Distinct from CCLA blue (#0ea5e9)

## Typography

### Logo Wordmark

**Style requirements:**
- Clean, modern sans-serif
- Strong, authoritative presence
- Tech-forward but not trendy
- Excellent legibility at small sizes

**Recommended fonts:**
- Inter (open-source, modern, corporate)
- Satoshi (distinctive, premium feel)
- Manrope (geometric, clean)
- Outfit (contemporary sans)
- Custom geometric sans (if budget allows)

### Text Treatment Options

1. **ReprobusAI** (compound word, equal weight)
2. **Reprobus AI** (separate words, "AI" in lighter weight or accent colour)
3. **REPROBUS** with "AI" as superscript
4. **Reprobus.ai** (domain-style suffix)

**Recommendation:** Option 1 or 2 for strong single brand identity

## Layout & Composition

### Logo Variations Needed

**Primary full logo:**
```
[Icon] ReprobusAI
        (wordmark)
```

**Horizontal lockup:**
```
[Icon] ReprobusAI
```

**Stacked:**
```
  [Icon]
ReprobusAI
```

**Icon only:**
```
[Icon]
(for app icons, favicons, social media)
```

**Wordmark only:**
```
ReprobusAI
(for situations where icon doesn't work)
```

### Sizing & Spacing

- **Minimum size:** Logo must be legible at 24px height
- **Clear space:** Minimum clear space = height of "R" in ReprobusAI
- **Proportions:** Icon and wordmark should balance (not overpowering each other)

## Design Principles

### Must achieve:
1. **Professional authority** - Corporate clients must trust this brand
2. **Visual distinction** - Immediately different from Car Crash Lawyer AI
3. **Protection theme** - Guardian/shield concept clear
4. **Modern tech** - AI/technology evident but not clichéd
5. **Scalable** - Works from favicon to billboard
6. **Memorable** - Distinctive enough to recall

### Should avoid:
1. Generic shield icons (overused in insurance/security)
2. Literal AI imagery (brain, robot, circuit board as primary icon)
3. Gradients (hard to reproduce consistently)
4. Thin lines (don't scale to small sizes)
5. Complexity (should work in single colour)
