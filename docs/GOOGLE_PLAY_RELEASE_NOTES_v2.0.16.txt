Version 2.0.16 - Support FAQ

Q: Why aren't photos required anymore during signup?
A: We want to make it easier to get started. You can now complete signup in under 5 minutes and add photos later from your dashboard when you're ready.

Q: Do I still need to upload photos?
A: Photos are highly recommended as they strengthen your insurance claim and can increase your payout by thousands of pounds. While not required to complete signup, we strongly encourage uploading them within 24-48 hours.

Q: Where can I upload photos after signup?
A: Log into your dashboard and you'll see clear prompts to upload any missing photos. The dashboard shows what you've uploaded and what's still needed.

Q: Will my claim be weaker without photos?
A: Photos of your vehicle in pristine condition before the accident are crucial evidence. They prove pre-incident value and help maximize your insurance settlement. Our Pro-Tip feature explains exactly why they matter.

Q: What if I uploaded photos during signup in the old version?
A: Nothing changes for you. Your photos are safely stored and already part of your claim documentation.

Q: Can I still upload photos during signup if I have them?
A: Absolutely! The photo upload page is still there - we've just made it optional so you can continue if you don't have photos immediately available.
