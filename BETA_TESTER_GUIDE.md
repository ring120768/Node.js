# Car Crash Lawyer AI - Beta Testing Guide

## 📱 Installation

1. **Download the APK**
   Click the download link in your invite email (45MB)

2. **Install on Android**
   - Open Downloads folder on your phone
   - Tap `CarCrashLawyerAI.apk`
   - If prompted: Enable "Install unknown apps" → Tap Install
   - Tap Open when complete

3. **Requirements**
   - Android 7.0 (Nougat) or newer
   - 45MB free storage
   - Internet connection

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I submit multiple test reports?**
A: Yes! Feel free to test as many times as you like.

**Q: Will I be charged?**
A: No - this is a free beta test.

**Q: Can I use my real accident details?**
A: Absolutely! The system is fully functional. Just remember this is beta - always keep your own copies of important documents.

**Q: Do I need to use real photos?**
A: For testing, any photos work. The system just needs to process images.

**Q: How long will my data be stored?**
A: Test data may be cleared periodically. Don't rely on the beta for permanent storage.

**Q: What if I get stuck on a page?**
A: Try the Back button, or refresh the app. Report persistent issues.

**Q: The PDF hasn't arrived after 5 minutes**
A: Check your spam/junk folder. Still missing? Email us with your test email address.
